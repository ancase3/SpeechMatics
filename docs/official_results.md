# Official Results

The official results of the **SpeechMatics** shared task are reported below.

For clarity, this page reports **one official run per participant and per task**. The official ranking is based on the **mean F1-score** obtained for each task.

In addition to the official ranking, we also provide **bootstrap 95% confidence intervals** for Precision, Recall and F1-score. These intervals are included to help interpret the robustness of the results, especially when the scores of different systems are very close. Therefore, while the ranking follows the mean F1-score, the confidence intervals should be considered when comparing systems with overlapping performance.

## Notes on the ranking

The official ranking is determined by the mean F1-score for each task. However, bootstrap confidence intervals are reported to provide a more informative comparison between systems.

When two systems obtain very similar mean F1-scores and their confidence intervals largely overlap, their relative ranking should be interpreted with caution. In such cases, the difference in performance may not indicate a clearly superior system.

The baseline is included as a reference system and is not considered a participant submission.
